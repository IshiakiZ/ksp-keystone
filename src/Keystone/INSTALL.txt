Keystone @VERSION@
for Kerbal Space Program 1.12.x

The base a family of small mods stands on: one window that lists every mod installed (the
mods of this family with their switches and settings, every other with what can be known of
it), settings that are kept between runs, keys you can change, a page of what each mod costs,
and a lens of its own (depth of field, motion blur, grain) for the mods that film.

INSTALL
  Copy the Keystone folder in this download into your KSP's GameData folder. One
  copy serves every mod that uses it; each of those mods' own downloads has a copy in it too.
  The page of what each mod costs needs the Harmony mod (HarmonyKSP); without it, the rest works.

USE
  The keystone button on the game's toolbar, or Option-K on a Mac and Alt-K elsewhere.
  Looking online for newer versions of your mods is off until you switch it on, at the foot of
  the list; nothing is ever downloaded or installed.

WHERE IT WORKS
  Made and tried on a Mac, and tried on Windows 11 (on the game's own Direct3D 11, and
  on OpenGL). What 0.7.2 changed (how the window is dragged, and its hold on the wheel
  while the pointer is over it) has been tried on the Mac only. Nobody has run it on
  Linux yet.
